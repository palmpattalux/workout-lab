# Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{updated}}

This policy explains what personal data Dotset collects, why, who helps us handle it, and the choices and rights you have. It is written to meet Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (PDPA) and the rules of the App Store and Google Play.

The data controller is {{owner}}, {{address}} ("we", "us"). Contact for anything about your data: {{email}}

## 1. The short version

- We collect only what Dotset needs to work: your email, what you log, and what you buy.
- No ads, no tracking, no selling or renting your data, and no third-party analytics.
- You can see, download, correct or delete your data at any time. Deleting your account erases it for good.

## 2. What we collect

- **Account:** your email address and a password. The password is stored only as a secure hash by our login provider; we can't read it.
- **What you log:** exercises (names, icons, equipment, notes on focus), sessions, sets, reps, weights or seconds, progressions, presets and notes you write.
- **Purchases:** which plan or items you bought, when, how you paid (card, PromptPay, TrueMoney, App Store or Google Play) and the amount. Card details go straight to our payment provider; we never see or store your card number.
- **Settings:** language, light or dark mode, sounds, theme and icon choice. These are kept on your device.
- **Technical data:** when you sign in, our hosting and login providers record basic logs (such as IP address, device and browser type, and time of access) to keep the service secure and to meet the Computer-Related Crime Act.

We don't ask for your name, birthday, location, contacts, photos, body measurements or health records.

**About notes:** please don't write medical information (such as illnesses, injuries or medication) in your notes. If you choose to, you give us your explicit consent to store it only so you can read it back, and you can delete it at any time.

## 3. Why we use it (and our legal basis)

- **To provide Dotset** (your account, syncing your workouts between devices, plans and purchases): this is needed to perform our agreement with you (PDPA s.24(3)). Without your email we can't make an account; you can still use Dotset as a guest on one device.
- **To send account emails** (verify your email, reset your password, payment receipts and important notices about your plan or these policies): needed for our agreement with you.
- **To keep Dotset secure and prevent fraud and abuse:** our legitimate interest (s.24(5)).
- **To keep payment and tax records:** a legal obligation (s.24(6)).
- **Marketing emails:** only if you say yes separately, and you can say no at any time. We don't send them today.

## 4. Who helps us (and where your data goes)

We use a few trusted providers to run Dotset. They process data only on our instructions and must keep it safe:

- **Supabase** (database and login), servers in Singapore or the United States.
- **Vercel** (website hosting and server functions), United States and global edge network.
- **Resend** (sending account emails), United States.
- **Opn Payments / Omise** (website payments), Thailand.
- **Apple and Google** (purchases made in the iPhone or Android app), under their own privacy policies.

Some of these are outside Thailand. We transfer data abroad only as the PDPA allows: because it is needed to provide Dotset to you under our agreement, and with data protection contract terms (including standard contractual clauses) offered by these providers. We don't share your data with anyone else, except when the law requires it (for example a valid court order), or to protect someone's safety.

## 5. On your device

Dotset saves your data on your device first so it opens instantly and works offline, then syncs it to your account. This uses your browser's or app's local storage, only for Dotset to work. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, so there is nothing to opt out of.

## 6. How long we keep it

- Your account and what you log: for as long as you have an account. If your plan ends, your data stays so you can come back to it. If you delete your account, it is erased within 30 days, including from backups.
- Payment records: kept by our payment provider and in our accounts for as long as Thai tax and accounting law requires (usually 5 years).
- Security logs: about 90 days, or longer if the law requires.

## 7. Your rights

Under the PDPA you can ask to:

- see and get a copy of your data, or have it sent to another service;
- correct data that is wrong;
- delete your data (or delete your account yourself: Settings > Delete account, or dotset.app/delete-account);
- object to or restrict how we use it;
- withdraw any consent you gave, without affecting what happened before.

Email {{email}} from your account's address. We reply within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC) of Thailand.

## 8. Children

Dotset is for people aged 13 and over. People under 20 need a parent's or guardian's permission to make an account and buy anything. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

## 9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), each account can only read its own data, and payment details are handled by a certified payment provider. No system is perfect: if a breach ever puts your data at risk, we will tell the PDPC within 72 hours and tell you without delay when the law requires it.

## 10. Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we will tell you in the app or by email before it applies. The date at the top shows the latest version.
