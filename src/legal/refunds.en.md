# Refund, Cancellation and Delivery Policy

Last updated: {{updated}}

Dotset sells digital items: plans (subscriptions), themes and icon sets.

## Delivery

There is nothing shipped. Everything you buy works in your account the moment your payment goes through, on every device where you log in. If something you paid for hasn't appeared within 15 minutes, email {{email}} and we will add it or refund you.

## Cancelling a plan

- **Card (website):** turn off renewing anytime in the app under Plan. You won't be charged again, and your plan stays active until the end of the month you already paid for.
- **PromptPay or TrueMoney (website):** these pay for one month and never renew, so there is nothing to cancel.
- **App Store or Google Play:** cancel in your Apple or Google account's subscription settings, at least 24 hours before the renewal date.
- Cancelling or letting a plan end never deletes your data. Extra features are hidden until you renew.

## When we refund (website purchases)

We want you to be happy, so we refund in full when:

- **Your first plan payment:** you ask within 7 days of your first payment for that plan. Your account goes back to Free.
- **A theme or icon set bought by mistake:** you ask within 7 days of buying it. The item is removed from your account.
- **You were charged after turning off renewing,** charged twice, or charged the wrong amount.
- **Something we sold doesn't work** and we can't fix it within a reasonable time.
- **A payment you didn't make,** or a purchase by someone under 20 without a parent's or guardian's permission, when the parent or guardian asks us.
- **We close Dotset or your account** for no fault of yours: we refund the plan time you paid for and haven't used.

Other than that, renewal payments and items used for more than 7 days aren't refundable, because they are digital and start working straight away. You can always turn off renewing so the next month isn't charged. This doesn't take away any right you have under Thai consumer law.

## How to ask for a refund

Email {{email}} from your account's address with the date and amount of the payment. We reply within 7 days. Approved refunds go back to the same card or payment method within 15 days; your bank or wallet may take a few more days to show it.

## App Store and Google Play purchases

Purchases made in the iPhone or Android app are billed by Apple or Google, so refunds follow their rules. Ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, or Google through Google Play (Order history). We can't refund those payments ourselves.
